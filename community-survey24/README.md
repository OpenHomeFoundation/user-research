# Home Assistant Community Survey 2024

This repo hosts the open dataset and survey questions from the [2024 Home Assistant Community Survey](https://www.home-assistant.io/blog/2024/12/16/community-survey-2024/), run by the Open Home Foundation.

For our first-ever community survey, we ran a large-scale survey of the Home Assistant Community, with an open dataset for everyone to use and learn from. Our goal is to share insights into smart home usage and its intersection with open source.
## Why we run this survey

The Open Home Foundation fights for privacy, choice, and sustainability in the smart home, and for every person who lives in one: control over your own data, devices that mix and match across manufacturers without vendor lock-in, and hardware built to last and be repurposed rather than thrown away.

As a movement, we know a lot about the software and standards we build, but very little about the people who actually live with them. What motivates people to use it, which features they actually rely on, what their homes look like, who they share them with, and how this technology fits into the social side of everyday life at home?

Living up to our mission means knowing who we're building for, regardless of who they are or where they come from. This survey is how we get to know the people behind Home Assistant better.

## Survey details

- Opened: December 16, 2024  
- Closed: January 20, 2025  
- Length: expected \~20 minutes, with a partial-submit checkpoint halfway through. Actual average: 45+ minutes. A heartfelt thank you to the Home Assistant Community for the time and care they put into this.

## Data handling & privacy

The survey is anonymous. All questions were optional, and sensitive questions additionally included a "prefer not to say" option. Data is handled according to GDPR standards; see the [User Research Agreement](https://www.openhomefoundation.org/user-research-agreement/) for details.

**Note on completeness of the published dataset:** This published dataset contains 7,572 of the 8,619 survey responses. The 1,047 omitted records (12.1%) were removed during anonymization because their combination of characteristics was rare enough to risk identifying an individual. Because the removed records are precisely those with uncommon profiles, the published data slightly under-represents unusual or rare configurations. Analyses concerning the prevalence of rare setups, or focused on small subgroups, should take this into account. Responses to open text field questions were also removed to ensure anonymization. The dataset is representative of the broad population of respondents but is not the complete raw sample.

## Survey questions

The full questionnaire is published in two forms:

* [`survey-questions.md`](https://github.com/OpenHomeFoundation/user-research/blob/main/community-survey24/survey-questions.md) \- the survey exactly as participants saw it, with all original question wording, descriptions and answer options. This includes questions that are *not* in the dataset, such as open text fields that were removed during anonymisation.  
* [`24_survey_questions.xlsx`](https://github.com/OpenHomeFoundation/user-research/blob/main/community-survey24/24_survey_questions.xlsx) \- the question set as it maps onto the published dataset, i.e. after the edits made under our [`Anonymisation Protocol & Decision Record.pdf`](https://github.com/OpenHomeFoundation/user-research/blob/main/community-survey24/Anonymisation%20Protocol%20%26%20Decision%20Record.pdf). Use this one to interpret the columns in the data file.
