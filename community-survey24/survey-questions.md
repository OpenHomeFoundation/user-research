# Home Assistant Community Survey 2024

Your responses are anonymous, and all questions are optional. Demographic questions about you and your home members help us better understand Home Assistant usage and the community.

We promise to handle the collected data as described in our [User Research Agreement](https://www.openhomefoundation.org/user-research-agreement/).

The survey takes around 20 minutes to complete. Your progress will be automatically saved.
